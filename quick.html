About

Why work with me.

I'm Dylan Shook. I build custom websites for small businesses out of Kokomo, Indiana — for authors, service operators, gaming retailers, and anyone whose business deserves more than a generic template.

Before I went freelance I worked IT helpdesk supporting hundreds of users on legacy infrastructure. That work taught me exactly why generic agency builds break for working business owners — they're built for the agency's convenience, not the client's. My sites are built clean, run fast, and don't require you to call me every time you need to update your hours.

I work with one trusted developer for backend and custom code work, but you'll always be talking to me directly. One point of contact. One timeline. One fixed price.

Questions

Things people ask.

What if I already have a website?+
I'll audit it for free and tell you whether it makes sense to rebuild or just clean up. Sometimes the answer is don't hire me — and that's a fine answer.
Do you do ongoing maintenance?+
Optional Care Plan at $99/month. Covers updates, backups, security patches, and up to one hour of monthly content edits. Cancel anytime, no contracts.
What platforms do you build on?+
WordPress for most clients (so you can update content yourself without calling me), Squarespace for simpler builds, or fully custom code if you need something specific. I recommend the right tool for the job, not the one that pads my margin.
Can I see examples of your work?+
Yes. Recent builds include author sites and gaming retail. Drop me a line and I'll send live links relevant to your industry.
What if I need changes after launch?+
Revisions are included in every tier (two to three rounds depending on tier). After launch, additional changes are billed at $75/hour, or roll into the Care Plan. No surprise invoices.
Are you a real business?+
Sole proprietor based in Indiana. Real name, real address, real phone number, invoiced through standard tools, paid via bank transfer or card. Happy to provide W-9 documentation for business clients.
What's the founding client pricing about?+
Twenty-five percent off any tier in exchange for a written testimonial and the right to use your project as a case study. Limited to the first five clients. After that, pricing returns to standard rates.
